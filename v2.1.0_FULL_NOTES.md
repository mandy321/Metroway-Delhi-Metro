This major update integrates live official DMRC service data, introduces a highly optimized UI, and enhances map interaction with live animations and haptics.

- **Official Alerts:** Live feed from the **official DMRC Status API**. 
- **Real-Time Reliability:** Instant updates on snags or delays across the entire network.

### 🗺️ Interactive Map Upgrades
- **Nearest Station Finder:** Auto-detects the closest metro station to your current GPS coordinates.

- **Haptic Arrival Buzz:** Triggers a distinct vibration alert when arriving at interchange hubs or your destination.

- **Ultra-Compact Layout:** Redesigned Home screen to maximize visible route information.

### ⚡ Performance & Polish
- **60fps Fluidity:** Bottom sheet gestures now use the native animation driver.
- **Slider Safety Lock:** Intelligent clamping ensures the drag handle remains reachable.
- **Zero-Lag Search:** Memoized station filtering for instant responsiveness.
